Privacy Policy

Abstract Adventures - Mahjong Solitaire

Effective 7 August 2026 · Last updated 7 August 2026

In short: this game collects nothing. It requests no Android permissions, contains no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party components that transmit information. Everything it remembers, such as your game in progress, your scores, your statistics, is stored only on your device, and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

2. Personal and sensitive data we collect

None. We do not access, collect, use, transmit, or share any personal or sensitive user data of any kind. Specifically, and without limitation, our applications do not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any other contact or identity information
  • Accounts or login credentials
  • Location data, whether precise or approximate
  • Device or advertising identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID
  • Contacts, calendar, photos, media, files, camera or microphone data
  • Installed application inventories
  • Usage analytics, telemetry, crash reports, or diagnostic information
  • Payment or financial information. The application contains no in-app purchases or payment functionality

3. Parties we share data with

None. Because we collect no user data, there is no user data to share, sell, rent, or otherwise disclose.

4. Information stored on your own device

To function as a game, our application saves data locally on your device. This includes items such as the game currently in progress, high scores, play statistics, and your settings and preferences.

This information stays in the application's private storage area on your device. It is never transmitted to us or to anyone else, and we have no ability to read it.

5. Data retention and deletion

We retain no user data, because we receive none. There are no servers holding information about you, no accounts, and no backend of any kind.

The locally stored game data described in section 4 remains on your device for as long as the application is installed, and is under your control at all times. You can erase this data at any time by clearing the application's storage through your device settings, or by uninstalling the application.

6. Secure handling of data

Because we do not receive or store user data, there are no databases, user accounts, or credential stores for us to secure.

7. Permissions

The application does not request any Android permissions.
